## Why virtual talent works for brands
- **Full creative control**: Wardrobe, locations, lighting, and styling are set to your brief.
- **Predictable scheduling**: Production runs to your calendar, without travel or availability constraints.
- **Consistent brand safety**: Character conduct and visual identity stay within agreed guidelines.
- **Clear reuse rights**: Licensing scope is defined up front, so you know where and how long assets can run.

## How we work
1. **Brief**: You share the product, the audience, the channels, and the campaign goal.
2. **Concept**: We propose looks, locations, and a shot list for approval.
3. **Production**: We produce the imagery and copy, and share drafts for review.
4. **Approval and disclosure**: Each asset carries AI disclosure, and affiliate or sponsored content is labeled before any link.
5. **Delivery and licensing**: Final files are delivered with usage terms agreed in writing.

## Our commitments
- **AI disclosure on every post**: Bios say the character is AI-generated, and the "Made with AI" label is switched on.
- **No impersonation**: We don't use the likeness of a real person without their written consent.
- **Clear sponsorship labels**: Paid partnerships and affiliate links are disclosed before the link.
- **Human review**: A person approves all brand-facing work before it goes out.
