## 7. Meta Anti-Spam Safety & Rate-Limit Safeguards

Instagram's automated spam detection algorithms flag rapid, repetitive, or identical commenting patterns. The Resrise engine implements bulletproof safeguards:

1. **Varied Reply Pools**: Every account utilizes 4+ distinct phrasing variations randomly selected per reply to ensure zero repetitive text signatures.
2. **Human Typing Cadence**: Playwright injects individual keystrokes with randomized delay (`45ms` to `80ms` per character), matching human speed.
3. **Action Spacing**: Post-submission pause of `3.0` to `5.0` seconds between replies, and `180` seconds between batch passes.
4. **Volume Ceiling**: Hard cap of **5 replies per post** and **5 posts per run** (maximum 25 replies per hourly window), far below Meta's 60 action/hour threshold.
5. **DOM Truth & Cache Poisoning Immunity**: The system inspects live DOM state for existing replies from the account before attempting to reply, preventing duplicate responses.

## 8. ASCI & FTC Legal Compliance Framework

All affiliate marketing activities conducted across Instagram and Telegram strictly adhere to the **Advertising Standards Council of India (ASCI) Guidelines for Influencer Advertising in Digital Media** and the **US FTC Guides Concerning Use of Endorsements**:

1. **Prominent Disclosure Placement**:
   - Disclosures must be clearly visible without requiring the user to click "See more" or expand collapsed text.
   - On Instagram comment replies, `#affiliate` is appended directly to the message.
   - On Telegram channel posts, each shopping link drop features `#affiliate` or `[Affiliate Link]`.
2. **Pinned Channel Disclosure**:
   - Every Telegram broadcast channel maintains a permanently pinned header detailing financial affiliate relationships.
3. **No Misleading Pricing**:
   - Saree, fashion, and grooming prices quoted in drops must represent current active prices on partner platforms (Myntra/Ajio/Amazon).
4. **Zero Counterfeit Endorsement**:
   - "Dupes" must be legitimate branded alternative designs (e.g., high-street alternatives by Mango, Zara, or verified Indian handlooms) and never counterfeit or trademark-infringing replicas.
