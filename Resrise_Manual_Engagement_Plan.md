# Resrise Studio: Manual Engagement Plan

**Scope**: Five accounts: @mairamehraofficial, @avanyamehra, @iamgagankumar, @gagankumarrocks, @resrise.  
**Principle**: Every like, comment, reply, follow, and DM is written or chosen by a person. No automated engagement, bulk following, or scripted replies.

## 1. Daily routine (about 20 minutes per account)
- Reply to every comment on your own posts within 24 hours. Write each reply yourself, and make it specific to the comment.
- Engage with 5 to 8 accounts in your niche. Leave a genuine comment on each, not "Nice!" or emoji-only replies.
- Post or share one Story a day, with a poll, a question sticker, or a link to the latest post.
- Check Direct messages once in the morning and once in the afternoon. Reply personally.

## 2. Weekly rhythm (per account)
| Day | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Monday** | Plan the week: pick 3 posts and 1 reel. Write captions. |
| **Tuesday** | Publish the first post (scheduled through Meta Business Suite, a native feature). Post a Story. |
| **Wednesday** | Engage with the posts of 2 collaborators or peer accounts. |
| **Thursday** | Publish a reel or carousel. Reply to all comments. |
| **Friday** | Publish the third post. Review the week's saves and shares. |
| **Weekend** | Post a Story. Reply to comments. No new posting. |

**Posting target**: 3 feed posts and 1 reel per week per account. Quality over volume.

## 3. Content mix
Keep affiliate posts to no more than 1 in 4. The rest should give value:
- **Styling guides**: how to wear a look, for an occasion or budget.
- **Behind-the-scenes**: the studio, the process, and how a shoot is built.
- **Tutorials (@gagankumarrocks)**: prompts, workflows, and studio setup.
- **Campaign case studies (@resrise)**: approved client work, with permission.
- **Affiliate picks**: a product you actually recommend, with clear disclosure.

## 4. Disclosure rules for every affiliate or sponsored post
- Put the disclosure at the top of the caption, before any link: `"#ad • Links in bio are affiliate links. I may earn a commission on qualifying purchases."`
- Add "Made with AI" for virtual characters, and "AI-assisted edit" for edited real photos.
- Use "Amazon Associate" wording on any page or channel that carries Amazon links: `"As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases."`
- Put the link in the bio link (⤵) or a link sticker, not raw in comments.

## 5. Driving traffic to Telegram
- Mention the channel naturally in captions, for example: `"Full edit in the Saree Club, link in bio."`
- Answer questions manually in comments. Example: `"Yes, that's from the Saree Club drop. I've linked it in my bio."`
- Don't ask people to comment a keyword to get a link. That's a trigger for automated replies, and we don't use them.

## 6. Cross-account collaboration
- Collaborate on posts only when the primary account owns the post and tags the others as collaborators.
- Reply to comments on shared posts from the primary account only, so the same person doesn't get duplicate replies.
- Reference other accounts in captions manually, with a reason, for example: `"Gagan's tailoring tips pair well with this look."`

## 7. Weekly review (30 minutes, Friday)
Track these for each account:
- Saves and shares, which signal the strongest content.
- Profile visits and bio link clicks (from Instagram Insights).
- Telegram joins and where they came from.
- Affiliate clicks and earnings from Amazon Associates and EarnKaro, by sub-ID.
- Comments answered within 24 hours, as a percentage.
Use the results to repeat what worked and drop what didn't. Don't chase follower counts alone.

## 8. Things we don't do
- No automated likes, comments, follows, story views, or DMs.
- No engagement pods or bought followers.
- No posting of ads or sponsored content without disclosure.
- No real person's likeness without written consent.
- No comment templates pasted across many posts.
