## 🚀 Step 1: EarnKaro (60 Seconds)
*Monetizes: Myntra, Ajio, Nykaa, Meesho, Tata CLiQ & Flipkart (Best for Fashion & Sarees)*

1. **Sign Up (Free)**:
   - Go to [EarnKaro.com](https://earnkaro.com) or download the EarnKaro App.
   - Sign up with your mobile number or email (`contact.resrise@gmail.com` or personal).

2. **Add Your Bank Account**:
   - Go to **My Profile ➔ Payment Settings ➔ Add Bank Details**.
   - Enter your **Bank Name, Account Number, and IFSC Code**.
   - Enter your **PAN Card number** for automated TDS deduction (1% TDS refundable in ITR).

3. **How Payouts Work**:
   - Commissions track as "Pending" when an item is ordered.
   - Once the return/exchange period ends (usually 30 days), the status turns to **"Confirmed"**.
   - As soon as your confirmed earnings reach **₹10**, tap **"Request Payment"** — funds are sent directly to your bank account via NEFT.

*Monetizes: Amazon.in (Suits, Blazers, Grooming Kits, Perfumes, Watch Dupes & Studio Tech)*

1. **Sign Up (Free)**:
   - Visit [affiliate-program.amazon.in](https://affiliate-program.amazon.in) and click **Sign Up**.
   - Log in with your standard Amazon.in shopping account.

2. **Website & Profile List**:
   - When asked for "Websites and Mobile Apps", paste your Instagram profile URLs:
     - `https://instagram.com/mairamehraofficial`
     - `https://instagram.com/avanyamehra`
     - `https://instagram.com/iamgagankumar`
     - `https://instagram.com/resrise`
   - Primary category: Select *Apparel / Shoes / Jewelry* or *Beauty / Grooming*.

3. **Payment & Tax Setup**:
   - Select **"Pay me by NEFT"** (Direct Deposit to Bank).
   - Enter your Indian **Bank Account Number, IFSC code, and PAN card**.
   - Minimum payout is **₹1,000** (auto-deposited every month on the 28th-30th).

4. **Your Associate Tag**:
   - Amazon will assign you a Primary Associate Tag (e.g., `resrise-21` or `yourname-21`).
   - You can also create separate tracking IDs under *Account Settings ➔ Manage Tracking IDs* (`mairamehra-21`, `avanyamehra-21`, `iamgagankumar-21`) to track revenue per persona.
