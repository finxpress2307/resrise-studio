## 7. ASCI & FTC Compliance & Meta Anti-Spam Safety Playbook

### 7.1 ASCI (Advertising Standards Council of India) Statutory Guidelines
As per ASCI Guidelines for Influencer Advertising in Digital Media (updated 2023) and Department of Consumer Affairs (DoCA) regulations, all commercial, affiliate, and sponsored partnerships must be explicitly disclosed:

1. **Mandatory Disclosure Labels**:
   - Every commercial post, affiliate product matching guide, and paid integration must include clear labels: `#ad`, `#affiliate`, `#collab`, or `#sponsored`.
   - Vague or hidden abbreviations (e.g., `#sp`, `#spon`, `#aff`, `#collabo`) are strictly prohibited.
2. **Prominent Above-the-Fold Placement**:
   - In Instagram feed posts, the disclosure label must appear within the first two lines of the caption (before the "more" ellipsis) or immediately adjacent to the primary CTA.
   - It must never be buried beneath a block of 20+ generic hashtags.
3. **Video / Reel Disclosure Standard**:
   - For all 9:16 vertical video assets (Reels), a visible text overlay reading `Paid Partnership` or `#ad / Affiliate Links` must be superimposed on the video for a minimum duration of **3 consecutive seconds**.
4. **Story Frame Disclosures**:
   - Each individual Instagram Story frame featuring an affiliate link sticker must feature a legible `#affiliate` or `Sponsored / Ad` tag directly beside the link sticker.
5. **Telegram VIP Channel Disclosures**:
   - Every Telegram broadcast containing monetization links must conclude with an explicit disclaimer:  
     `📢 ASCI / Affiliate Disclosure: Links above are affiliate links. When you purchase via these links, Maira earns an affiliate referral commission at no additional cost to you (#ad #affiliate).`

### 7.2 Meta Anti-Spam Architecture & Ban-Safety Rules
To protect `@mairamehraofficial` from Instagram action blocks, shadowbans, or algorithmic suppression, all automated activities through `scripts/ig_replier.py` and ManyChat must strictly adhere to these hard technical constraints:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│             META AUTOMATION SAFETY MATRIX              │
├───────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────┤
│ Hourly Comment Reply Cap      │ Maximum 25–30 / hour   │
│ Daily Action Ceiling          │ Strict maximum 150/day │
│ Randomized Action Jitter Delay│ 20 to 50 seconds delay │
│ Human Typing Simulation       │ 45 to 80 ms/keystroke  │
│ Spintex Diversity Pool        │ Minimum 30 variants    │
│ Identical Consecutive Actions │ 0 (Strict Zero Rule)   │
│ Account Cooldown Trigger      │ 12-hour freeze on block│
└───────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Hourly & Daily Caps**:
   - **Hourly Limit**: Under no circumstance may automated comment responses or DM deliveries exceed **30 actions per hour**.
   - **Daily Ceiling**: Hard stop at **150 automated actions per 24-hour cycle**. If a post goes viral and receives 500+ comments in an hour, remaining replies are queued and paced over subsequent hours.
2. **Randomized Jitter Delays**:
   - Between each comment response and outbound DM, an intentional random delay between **20.0 and 50.0 seconds** is enforced (`time.sleep(random.uniform(20.0, 50.0))`).
   - Robotic bursts (e.g. 5 replies within 10 seconds) are blocked by design.
3. **Spintex & Lexical Diversity Engine**:
   - As documented in Section 4.1, thirty distinct reply templates are dynamically sampled using uniform random distribution.
   - The same reply string is never dispatched twice within any consecutive 10-reply sequence.
4. **Human Typing Simulation**:
   - Automated text injection simulates natural keyboard typing cadences of **45ms to 80ms per character**, including random micro-pauses (150ms–350ms) before sending.
5. **Circuit Breaker & Checkpoint Handling**:
   - If Instagram returns HTTP 429 (Too Many Requests), a Challenge/Checkpoint prompt, or an action block error, the replier engine enters an immediate **12-hour silent sleep** and alerts Resrise Studio operations via email/Telegram.
